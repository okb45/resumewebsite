Multidisciplinary Senior Design

This senior design project focuses on developing a distance-detection and warning system to assist visually impaired users while riding bikes, scooters, or other mobility devices. The system is designed to identify obstacles in the user’s path and provide timely warnings, improving safety and independence in outdoor environments.

The project is a collaboration between students from multiple engineering disciplines, including computer, mechanical, and electrical engineering. This interdisciplinary approach brings together expertise in hardware integration, software development, and human-centered design, ensuring the solution is both technically sound and user-friendly.

At the core of the system is an NVIDIA Jetson paired with an Intel RealSense camera, which together provide the processing power and depth sensing needed to detect obstacles in real time. The Jetson processes visual and depth data, while the software under development will determine obstacle proximity and trigger appropriate feedback mechanisms for the rider.
